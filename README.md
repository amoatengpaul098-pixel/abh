# African Business Hub (ABH) — Professional v6

ABH v6 expands the platform into an African business ecosystem for discovery, commerce, opportunities, funding, procurement, networking and growth.

## v6 modules
- Business Opportunities: tenders, contracts, procurement, partnerships, investors and franchises
- Funding Hub: grants, loans, investor opportunities, startup competitions and eligibility matching
- B2B Marketplace: suppliers, manufacturers, wholesalers, bulk purchasing and RFQ flows
- ABH Nearby: nearby businesses, services, deals, events and service-area readiness
- Business Growth Center: sales/customer/profile/product metrics, recommendations and category/competitor signals
- Reputation Center: verification, ratings, reviews, responses and anti-spam workflow foundation
- ABH Advertising: promotion for products, services, events, deals, jobs and featured profiles
- Deals & Loyalty: coupons, points, referrals, memberships and business rewards
- Professional Network: business/professional profiles, follows, networking, posts and recommendations
- Africa Explorer: country → city → industry discovery, cross-border opportunities and import/export connections
- Multi-currency and multi-language readiness

## Technology
HTML + CSS + JavaScript + Firebase foundation. No Python.

## Production notes
The front-end flows are designed for Firebase integration. Live payments, identity/business verification, location services, rewards, advertising billing, investor matching and financial transactions require secure server-side workflows, approved providers and appropriate legal/compliance review.
